# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want participation in Maker Track Studio to be respectful and constructive.
Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being considerate of different experiences, backgrounds and viewpoints.
- Giving and receiving feedback with respect and focusing on the work.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting their impact.
- Sharing only information that is appropriate for a public project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, doxxing, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive
conduct in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests and other spaces when
someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content, close discussions, or restrict participation in response to conduct
that violates these standards.

Report concerns privately through GitHub's contact options or the private
reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers will explain the reason for
any enforcement action when doing so does not expose a reporter or affected
person to further harm.
